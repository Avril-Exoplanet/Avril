# Community code of conduct

Avril is a scientific software project. Contributors and users are expected to make the community accurate, respectful, and safe for people at every career stage.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, methods, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Credit prior work, data providers, reviewers, and contributors.
- Respect embargoes, unpublished candidates, private reports, and personal data.
- Welcome reproducible criticism and questions from researchers, students, and citizen scientists.
- Avoid overstating a transit candidate as a confirmed or newly discovered planet.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of results, credential disclosure, plagiarism, or attempts to pressure maintainers into hiding scientific limitations are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the project and its participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
